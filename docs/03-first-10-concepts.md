## Why #1 is the pilot

- **Fastest hook.** The rule is 11 words, universal and instantly creepy. There's no context needed.
- **The cleanest binary choice**, and both answers are defensible (it's _probably_ not Leo, but what if the hospital text is the fake?). That ambiguity is what produces arguments in comments.
- **The lowest production cost.** One apartment set, one hallway, a phone UI and two characters.
- **It sets up the series** (the Notice, Mia, Leo) without lore.
- The earlier Mia "Do you open the door?" concept is reused in a tighter form, so the existing exploration isn't wasted.
